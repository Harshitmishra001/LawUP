LawUP 5-Stage Multi-Agent Architecture

How contract provisions move through ingestion, fine-tuned LoRA models, semantic grounding, and bidirectional entailment verification.

1
Ingestion Agent

Extracts text from PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD. Cleans redactions and segments into structured clause units.

2
Simplifier Agent

SmolLM3-3B QLoRA model produces high-clarity plain English translations without changing legal meaning.

3
Classification Agent

Multi-label risk classifier (0.73 micro F1 on held-out CUAD) categorizing 11 distinct contract hazards.

4
Grounding Retriever

MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings matching flagged clauses against 60+ curated statutes and standards (76.9% top-1).

5
Dual Verifiers

Stage 5a: Bidirectional NLI meaning check. Stage 5b: Citation verifier ensuring grounding references strictly match classified risks.
